# TapeGrade: three-minute demo recording kit

Lead with one manager's decision: **find an opportunity → inspect the evidence → review a roster move**. Use the clearly labeled fictional sample for the complete ADD workflow, then a saved real scan to demonstrate identity checks, automatic excerpt fallback, and a withheld recommendation.

This is a recording plan and voiceover script, not a rendered video. The timeline is exactly 3:00. Record each scene separately and assemble them; processing time and navigation between scenes are edited out. Keep sample/live labels visible throughout.

## Verbatim voiceover

Read at roughly 130–140 words per minute, leaving room for the visual holds. The standalone narration is also in `DEMO_VOICEOVER.txt`.

### 0:00–0:12 — The hook

Your box score tells you what happened. TapeGrade helps you scout the next opportunity, inspect the film, and review a move for your roster.

### 0:12–0:32 — Your league, your evidence

Connect your Sleeper league to bring in your roster and scoring. Recent game logs show sourced fantasy averages and minutes, with missing data called out. League setup keeps scoring rules, player identities, and transaction eligibility open for review.

### 0:32–0:47 — Give it a mission

For the complete walkthrough, I'm switching to a fictional sample with simulated models. I ask, “Find me an undervalued guard.” TapeGrade builds a shortlist and a report for each candidate.

### 0:47–1:22 — The centerpiece

Here's Cameron. I can play the clip, scrub through it, and toggle the player, defender, and ball overlays. The tracking audit explains how the evidence was checked. In this simulated example, the first pass swaps identities and scores forty-six percent. The critic specifies the cream jersey, number four, and requests another tracking pass. The corrected result reaches ninety-one percent. SAM tracks the scene; Muse Spark checks the evidence and reasons about the decision.

### 1:22–1:43 — Evidence to verdict

Cameron's sample report shows defender distance in screen pixels and seventy-five percent ball proximity. The methodology and source frames are inspectable. These are limited film proxies; unsupported measurements stay unavailable. Cameron earns ADD, Jalen MONITOR, and Nico PASS. The sample move is Cameron for Theo, at eight dollars.

### 1:43–2:02 — Save and share

I can bookmark Jalen for follow-up, copy the move summary, and download a Tape Card plus the evidence JSON. Cards combine artwork with exact report text and measurements. Muse Image supplies artwork in supported live runs; this sample artwork is simulated.

### 2:02–2:24 — Prove the guardrails

My rules still decide what clears review: minimum confidence, a maximum bid, scoring weights, and protected players. Watch what happens when I protect Theo and save. The existing recommendation is withdrawn immediately. TapeGrade stages the decision for review. I make any actual roster move in Sleeper.

### 2:24–2:38 — The rest of the workspace

The film library accepts player-linked clips. History keeps reports searchable. Appearance supports light and dark, and local scheduling is available when its worker is enabled and the Mac stays awake.

### 2:38–2:54 — Show the real boundary

This saved real scan checked two excerpts, corroborated Hauser's identity, and surfaced player-specific statistics. Tracking remained too weak, so measurements and a move were withheld. The evidence determines the outcome.

### 2:54–3:00 — Close

TapeGrade. Scout the opportunity. Inspect the evidence. Make the call.

## Recording preparation

### Capture the live scenes first

1. Record the already connected workspace: **My roster**, the source-backed game rows in **League setup → Game logs**, and short inserts of the other setup tabs. Showing an existing connection is enough; a username-entry sequence spends time without demonstrating the result.
2. In **Scan history**, search for **Review Sam Hauser using recent ESPN footage**. Choose the completed report whose mission asks to automatically evaluate the alternative. The observed report shows **4 live calls**, **Automatically checked 2 excerpts**, **Identity corroborated**, and **45% tracking quality**. It has no supported move.
3. Prepare three short cuts from that report: excerpt checks; dated jersey/roster identity evidence; **Players in this clip** and the withheld recommendation. Sam Hauser, Franz Wagner, and Jamal Cain have their own statistics/availability cards. Franz's **Trade research** label means he is on another team's roster, not available as a free-agent add.
4. Capture the public footage's attribution and date in context. The two alternatives are excerpts of the **same highlight/play**, not independent game samples.
5. Use these saved results. A new paid scan is unnecessary for this recording. Do not show the sample ADD as a result from the real league.

### Prepare a separate sample recording database

The standard ports are already occupied by the existing app. When ready to record the sample, stop the existing stack from the terminal that owns it before starting the demo. Do not run two stacks on the same ports.

From the project root:

```bash
DATABASE_URL="sqlite:///./data/demo-recording-$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M%S).db" bash scripts/dev-demo.sh
```

This creates a separate timestamped SQLite database and starts the explicit mock profile. It leaves the real league database and `.env` intact. Generated media uses the existing ignored cache directories. Open `http://localhost:5173` after startup and confirm **Demo models**, **Sample league**, and **Run sample scan** before recording.

1. Keep sample defaults: **75% minimum confidence**, **$12 maximum FAAB bid**, default scoring, and the original three protected players. **Theo Martin must be unprotected initially.**
2. Run **Find me an undervalued guard.** once before recording. The first run may take longer while preparing film. Warm runs reuse cached operations.
3. Confirm the anchor results: Cameron ADD, Jalen MONITOR, Nico PASS; Cameron's audit 46% then 91%; nearest defender 117.4 px; on-ball involvement 75%; add Cameron/drop Theo/$8.
4. Download and capture the original card **before** changing protections. The download is a saved scan artifact; do not present the original card as an updated post-withdrawal recommendation.
5. Capture the mission and progress as a separate shot using **Start another scan**. A short **processing time shortened** caption makes the jump to the completed report clear. Do not pretend the whole scan completes instantly.
6. Record the guardrail withdrawal last. To repeat that scene, unprotect Theo, save, and run a new sample scan first. Unprotecting him alone does not restore a withdrawn move.
7. For the upload shot, show the input form without uploading real athlete footage to a fictional player's record. Uploaded footage replaces selected sample sources and mock identity checks reject real footage. Use a different, non-shortlisted sample player if an actual upload needs demonstrating.

### Capture and editing details

- Record at **1920 × 1080** if available, with readable browser zoom and consistent framing. Use close-ups for the audit percentages, recommendation, and identity evidence.
- Record screen clips first and voiceover separately. Most shots need only 3–8 seconds; retain a few extra seconds on either end for trimming.
- Scroll inside **Selected player report** for film, methodology, recommendation, audit, and downloads. Its content can scroll independently from the candidate list.
- Keep two persistent chapter labels: **SAMPLE · fictional players / simulated models** and **LIVE · saved real API report**. The sample correction is scripted simulation; the live example demonstrates actual checks and abstention.
- Use simple cuts and modest zooms. Keep the critic loop as the longest scene. Put source detail and minor controls in inserts instead of reading tables aloud.
- Keep a small model-role caption during the relevant scenes: **SAM 3.1: segmentation · Muse Spark: verification / critique / decisions · Muse Image: artwork**. Live evidence cards may use local artwork when film quality is insufficient.
- Show the scheduler's unavailable state honestly in the current environment. Do not toggle it on or suggest hosted/background operation independent of this Mac.
- Appearance can be shown as two separately captured views. A full mobile view is an optional 1–2 second insert replacing an appearance shot, not extra runtime.
- Add captions, keep background music low, and export **H.264 MP4, 1080p, 30 fps, 3:00 maximum**. Check the final export's duration rather than relying only on the edit timeline.

## Rehearsal verification

The sample path was rehearsed against the current code with an isolated temporary database and mock models. It completed with **zero live model calls**, produced all three expected verdicts and the $8 Cameron/Theo proposal, returned both PNG and evidence JSON, and withdrew the proposal after Theo was protected. The existing live UI was inspected separately; no new live scan was started for this plan.
